Paperdesk privacy

Updated 31 October 2026

Publisher action before publication: confirm the legal seller identity and review the contact and retention terms. Review this policy against the final released app and the website host’s practices.

Your library stays on your device

Paperdesk stores imported PDF copies, document notes, annotations, reading positions, collections, drawing notes, and recognized-text caches in its local app storage. The app does not provide a publisher-operated account, document server, advertising, or analytics service.

Local processing

PDF search, text recognition, and extractive study run on your device. Optional on-device study uses Apple’s local system model when available. The app does not send your questions or source passages to a publisher server or a third-party model API. Apple manages the availability and installation of its system models through your device settings.

Preferences and file metadata

The app saves preferences such as drawing input and study mode in its own settings. It checks modification metadata of its own library files to keep document text caches consistent. These values are not used for advertising or cross-app tracking.

Files you choose to share

Importing creates a local copy; your selected original remains separate. You choose where exported PDFs, notes, and backups are saved. A destination such as iCloud Drive or another file provider may transfer that file under that provider’s own terms. Paperdesk does not automatically synchronize its library. Exported files and backups are not encrypted by Paperdesk.

Retention and removal

Local content remains until you remove it or clear the app’s stored data. Removing a document from the visible library keeps a local recovery copy; it is not permanent erasure. Original files, exported files, backups, device backups, and provider-managed copies remain separate and must be managed at their respective locations. Active-library backups do not include the local recovery archive.

Support and operating system services

If you choose to send a support request, include only information you are comfortable sharing. Avoid sending private documents. Support and privacy questions can be sent to kvnavneet@gmail.com. Messages and attachments are handled through that email service; Navneet must confirm the support-data retention policy before this page is published. Apple’s operating system diagnostics and a website host’s access logs are governed by their respective settings and policies; this static page contains no analytics scripts or remote fonts.
